PrayAlong Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

In short: PrayAlong is for praying with your friends. Your prayers are seen only by you and the friends you have added who added you back, or only by you if you post privately. We don't sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

PrayAlong is published by Joel Pang ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the PrayAlong app collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. If you have a question, email j.pang3248@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your account. You sign in with Apple or Google. We receive an account identifier and, depending on what you share with Apple or Google, your name and email address. If you use Sign in with Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive a private relay address instead.

Your profile. Your display name, username and an optional profile photo. Optionally, details only your friends can see: a short bio, your birthday, where you're from, your prayer requests, the Bible version you read, your denomination and your church.

What you post. Each prayer's photo, the words you write, when you posted (and when you pressed "Pray now", which only you can see), the day's verse reference, whether it's shared with friends or private, and, if you mark it answered, when and an optional note. If you choose to show where you prayed, we store only a town or city name, never your exact location.

Things you do with friends. Friend requests and friendships, comments, reactions, "I'll pray for this" taps on friends' requests, people you block, and reports you send us.

Your settings and saved verses. Your chosen Bible translation, and the references of verses you save (only the reference, never the text). If you turn on notifications about friends praying for your requests, reacting or commenting, we store which ones you chose, your language, and a random code your phone uses to receive them. We don't store push tokens.

What stays on your phone

Some things never leave your device: a draft of a prayer you're writing, prayers waiting to send when you're offline, and your display, language, voice and reminder preferences. "Listen" uses your phone's own voices. The lock screen countdown and the optional reminder are created on your phone. Friends' photos and details are kept in memory only while you use the app, never saved to your phone's storage. If you use Download my prayers, the file is made on your phone from your own prayers, photos and saved verses only (never friends' comments or reactions), and the app deletes its copy once you've saved or sent it; where it goes is your choice.

How we use it

  • To run PrayAlong: show your prayers to the people you've chosen, show you your friends' prayers, and keep your history in the Prayers tab.
  • To send the one daily "Time to pray" notification, if you allow notifications.
  • If you turn them on, to tell you when a friend prays for your requests, reacts to or comments on your prayer. These name the friend and go only to you.
  • To keep PrayAlong safe: review reports and act on blocks.

We don't use your information for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't build profiles about you. PrayAlong has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

Who can see what

  • Anyone signed in to PrayAlong can see your display name, username and profile photo, so friends can find you.
  • Your current friends (people you've added who added you back) can see your shared prayers, their photos, your friends-only details and your prayer requests. When a friendship ends, they lose access right away.
  • Only you can see your private prayers, your saved verses, your settings, and who is praying for your requests (unless you choose to show names, which also only you see).
  • Comments and reactions are seen by the post's author and by people who can see the post and are friends with the person who wrote them.
  • Blocks are private: the person you block isn't told.
  • Us: we can access the stored data to run, secure and support PrayAlong, and we read reports you send, which may quote the reported prayer or comment.

Service providers

We use these services to run PrayAlong, under their own privacy terms:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Hosting) stores your account and content and delivers notifications. It may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States.
  • Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple, if you use them to sign in.
  • Apple Push Notification service, which delivers notifications to iPhones.
  • Apple's location service, used on your phone to turn your position into a town name when you choose to show where you prayed. We never receive or store your position.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while you have an account. You can edit your profile and delete any of your prayers at any time; deleting a prayer also deletes its photo, comments and reactions.

You can delete your account in the app: Settings → Delete account. This immediately deletes your profile, prayers and photos, your comments, reactions and "I'll pray for this" taps (including those on friends' posts), your friendships and blocks, your saved verses and settings, the reports you sent, and your sign-in. Reports others made about you are kept for safety with the quoted text removed. Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a short time before they are overwritten.

Children

PrayAlong is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can turn off notifications, location and other permissions in your phone's Settings at any time. You can download a copy of your own prayers at any time: Settings → Download my prayers. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information. To use them, or for any privacy question, email j.pang3248@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its date. If the changes are significant, we'll let you know in the app.
